# Tone & Style Enforcer

## Use

Apply this when the user asks to review or write landing pages, ad copy, or website content and wants it to stay consistent with a brand voice, tone guide, or audience style.

## Workflow

1. Identify the target tone, audience, and brand rules.
2. Check the draft for voice drift, jargon, inconsistency, or mismatched formality.
3. Rewrite to match the requested tone while preserving meaning.
4. Keep clarity, rhythm, and formatting tight.
5. If tone rules are missing, infer a sensible voice from the existing copy and stay consistent.

## Writing rules

- Match the stated or implied brand voice.
- Preserve audience fit first, cleverness second.
- Avoid tone shifts inside the same piece.
- Keep claims clear and concrete.
- Favor concise, readable language.

## Output shape

- Revised copy, or
- Review notes with tone issues and recommended fixes, or
- Side-by-side before/after when helpful

## Good fits

- Landing pages
- Ad copy
- Email and campaign copy
- Client website content
- Brand voice edits

## Constraints

- Do not invent a new brand voice unless asked.
- Do not over-edit if the existing tone is already working.
- Keep the content natural, not template-stiff.
