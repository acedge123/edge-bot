# Output Templates

Use these lightweight structures and adapt them to the task.

## 1. Method recommendation

### Situation
- Business context
- Decision to support
- Constraints

### Options
For each option:
- method
- sample / source
- timeline
- strengths
- weaknesses
- confidence level
- relative cost

### Recommendation
- preferred design
- why it wins
- assumptions
- key risks
- next step

## 2. Research brief

### Objective
### Target audience
### Key questions / hypotheses
### Recommended methodology
### Sample / recruit approach
### Fieldwork plan
### Deliverables
### Risks and assumptions
### Confidence label
### Decision the work will unlock

## 3. Proposal / SOW draft

### Scope summary
### Workstreams or phases
### Methodology
### Sample / recruiting plan
### Deliverables
### Timeline
### Team / roles
### Assumptions
### Exclusions
### Risks / dependencies
### Pricing notes or range
### Approval checkpoints

## 4. Critique of a proposed study

### What works
### What is weak or risky
### Methodology mismatches
### Sample / timing / budget concerns
### Better alternatives
### Recommended revision

## 5. Insight synthesis

### Executive takeaway
### What we learned
### Tensions / contradictions
### What appears directional vs robust
### Implications for brand / product / activation
### Recommended next tests or actions

## Tone rules

- Sound like a senior strategist, not a hype machine.
- Prefer plain language over jargon unless the audience expects research terminology.
- Keep claims proportional to evidence.
- If assumptions are unverified, label them clearly.

## Good phrasing patterns

- "This design is best if the decision is..."
- "The tradeoff is speed versus confidence..."
- "Current sample would be directional, not decision-grade..."
- "If budget is fixed, the cleanest cut is..."
- "This approach will answer X well, but not Y..."

## Avoid

- "statistically significant" without a real basis
- fake precision on timing or incidence
- proposal filler with no decision value
- writing that hides tradeoffs
