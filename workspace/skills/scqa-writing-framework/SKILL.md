# SCQA Writing Framework

## Use

Turn rough ideas into structured content with a tight narrative arc, especially when writing or revising marketing copy, reports, threads, or explanations.

## Workflow

1. Identify the current context.
2. Surface the tension, gap, or problem.
3. State the natural question the audience will have.
4. Deliver the answer clearly and concretely.
5. Keep transitions smooth and concise.

## Output shape

Use this order:

- **Situation**: establish the baseline
- **Complication**: introduce what changed or what is blocked
- **Question**: frame the core question
- **Answer**: resolve with insight, recommendation, or next step

## Writing rules

- Keep sentences short.
- Avoid jargon unless the audience expects it.
- Do not skip sections.
- Do not repeat the same idea across sections.
- Prefer direct, readable prose over cleverness.

## Good fits

- Threads
- Articles
- Reports
- Explanations
- Decision memos
- Strategy summaries

## Output format

Default to short paragraphs or SCQA blocks with optional bullets under Answer when clarity improves.
