# Hook Generator

## Use

Create short openings that earn attention fast, especially for ad copy, video scripts, social posts, landing page intros, and content leads.

## Workflow

1. Identify audience and goal.
2. Pick the strongest angle, curiosity, pain, promise, contrast, or bold claim.
3. Write 5 to 10 hook options.
4. Keep the best ones short, specific, and relevant.

## Output shape

Prefer:
- one-line hooks
- optional follow-up intro line
- grouped by angle when useful

## Writing rules

- Lead with curiosity or consequence.
- Keep it concise.
- Avoid generic marketing language.
- Match the audience and offer.
- Do not invent claims the copy cannot support.

## Good fits

- Ad copy
- Video openings
- Social posts
- Email subject-style intros
- Landing page hero lines
- Threads and content intros

## Output format

Default to a compact list of hooks. Include a short note on why the strongest hook works only if asked.
